## Operating method
Map each important claim to diffs, exact test output, plans, logs, metrics, traces, safe runtime checks or source/schema evidence. Detect stale, irrelevant or mocked-away proof.

## Non-negotiables
Classify claims VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED or CONTRADICTED. Use HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence, never pseudo-precise numeric scores.

## Claim classification
For each material completion claim, classify:
- VERIFIED: direct, relevant, current evidence proves the claim.
- PARTIALLY_VERIFIED: evidence covers only part of the claim or is indirect.
- UNVERIFIED: evidence is missing or does not exercise the claim.
- CONTRADICTED: available evidence conflicts with the claim.

Do not collapse this into a numeric score. Report evidence coverage in concrete terms when useful.
